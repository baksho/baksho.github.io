## 2. Overview

This is a personal academic website. It has no user accounts, contact forms, comment function, advertising or web analytics. To my knowledge it does not set any cookies.

## 3. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. and its affiliates (USA). When you open a page, your browser sends technical data to GitHub's servers, in particular your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested URL, and browser and device information. GitHub processes this data to deliver the site and to keep it secure and may keep it in server logs. I do not have access to these logs as a visitor list.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing and securing a website). Data may be processed in the USA. Details on GitHub's processing and the safeguards it relies on are in [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 4. Scripts and styles loaded from content delivery networks

To display the site (for example the navigation, search, maths and image zoom), some open-source libraries are loaded from the content delivery networks jsDelivr (`cdn.jsdelivr.net`) and cdnjs (`cdnjs.cloudflare.com`, operated by Cloudflare). When a page loads, your browser contacts these services, which therefore receive your IP address and standard request data. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a reliably working, fast website). Please see the privacy information of the respective providers.

The site's fonts are system fonts of your device; no web fonts are loaded from third parties.

## 5. Data stored in your browser

The site stores a few settings on your device only; they are not sent to me or to any third party:

- your light/dark/system theme choice (local storage), so that the site opens in the theme you picked;
- a marker for the introduction on the About page and a random number that shapes the landscape graphic (session storage, deleted when you close the tab).

These are used solely to provide the function you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG), so no consent banner is required. You can delete them at any time in your browser settings.

## 6. Contacting me by email

If you write to me, I process your email address and the content of your message to answer your request. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (and Art. 6(1)(b) if the request concerns a contract or pre-contractual measures). I delete the correspondence once the matter is resolved and no retention duties apply.

## 7. Links to other websites

This site links to external services such as Google Scholar, GitHub, LinkedIn, ORCID, Medium and arXiv. Nothing is transferred to them until you click a link; after that, their own privacy policies apply.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can contact me about these at the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one responsible for your place of residence or for my place of residence (Lower Saxony: Die Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen).

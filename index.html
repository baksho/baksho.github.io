Suvinava Basak

Data Scientist · Machine Learning · Computational BiologyData Scientist · Machine Learning · Computational Biology

Braunschweig, Germany

I am a statistical machine learning researcher interested in the mathematical and empirical foundations of deep learning: generalization, optimization, uncertainty quantification, calibration and learning under distribution shift. I combine theoretical analysis of neural-network behavior with computational experiments, and apply it to biomedical machine learning.

I am an M.Sc. student in Data Science at TU Braunschweig and am writing my thesis on end-to-end calibration of neural networks in a biomedical context, supervised by Prof. Dr. Nicole Mücke at the Institute for Statistics and Foundations of Machine Learning. I also work as a student research assistant at Medizinische Hochschule Hannover, where I develop an LLM- and knowledge-graph-aware multi-agent RAG system for scientific hypothesis generation from heterogeneous biomedical evidence.

Before my master’s I worked in industry for several years, first at Ericsson and then as a strategy consultant at Wipro, with an MBA from IIM Indore in between. I am a recipient of the Deutschlandstipendium (2025/26).

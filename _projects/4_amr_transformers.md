Benchmarked encoder-only pretrained transformer models, including DNABERT and Nucleotide Transformer, for binary antimicrobial resistance (AMR) prediction from _pbp4_ genomic sequences, and investigated representation learning and parameter-efficient adaptation of pretrained biological language models using PyTorch. Seminar "Deep Learning in Molecular Biology", supervised by Prof. Dr. Alice C. McHardy.

### Research motivation

Reliable uncertainty estimation is essential when machine learning models are applied to scientific problems where predictions may be made under conditions that differ from the training distribution. This project investigates the relationship between finite-width neural networks and their infinite-width Gaussian Process limits, with a focus on uncertainty estimates under distribution shift.

### Objectives

- Investigate uncertainty estimation through the connection between neural networks and Gaussian Processes.
- Compare uncertainty estimates from different modeling approaches.
- Evaluate empirical behavior under distribution shift.
- Examine the strengths and limitations of the different uncertainty estimation methods.

### Approach

I compared Neural Network Gaussian Processes (NNGPs), deep ensembles, Monte Carlo dropout, and RBF Gaussian processes, focusing on their empirical uncertainty behavior under distribution shift. The work is a comparative study of probabilistic and deep-learning approaches.

### Contribution and outcome

The project connects theoretical perspectives on neural network limits with practical uncertainty estimation methods. It produced a comparative investigation of uncertainty estimation methods and their empirical behavior under distribution shift, and strengthened my understanding of how neural-network limits relate to practical uncertainty estimation.

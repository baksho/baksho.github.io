Research Interests

Machine learning for biomarker discovery and clinical decision support in systems medicine. I am interested in integrating heterogeneous molecular, clinical and literature data, including transcriptomic and multi-omics information, to identify predictive biomarkers and therapeutic targets, with an emphasis on interpretable models and calibrated, uncertainty-aware predictions that remain reliable under distribution shift.

Research Experience

Apr 2026 – Oct 2026 Braunschweig, Germany

Master's Thesis

Institute for Statistics and Foundations of Machine Learning, TU Braunschweig

Advisor: Prof. Dr. Nicole Mücke

End-to-End Calibration: Theory and Empirical Evaluation in Biomedical Context

  • Investigated end-to-end calibration of neural networks, incorporating differentiable calibration objectives directly into model training rather than relying only on post-hoc calibration, and studying their effects on predictive reliability and uncertainty estimation.
  • Studied the relationship between predictive performance, model confidence and reliability under distribution shift, using Transformer-based gene regulatory network inference as a scientific application.
Aug 2025 – Ongoing Hannover, Germany

Student Research Assistant

CAIMed Research Group, Medizinische Hochschule Hannover, Germany

Advisors: Dr. rer. nat. Zahra Ahmadi, Dr.-Ing. Raneen Younis

Multi-agent System for Scientific Hypothesis Generation

  • Building an LLM- and Knowledge Graph-aware RAG-based multi-agent system that proposes biomarker-guided drug combination hypotheses from heterogeneous biomedical evidence, integrating molecular, genomic, clinical and literature information. Co-first author of the resulting ICDM 2026 demo-track paper.
  • Designing ReAct framework-based agentic reasoning, evidence-held-out-benchmark and Budgeted Evidence Acquition and Decision-making (BEAD) policy, and risk-controlled calibrated confidence for each hypothesis, so the system signals how reliable each hypothesis is when evidence is incomplete or heterogeneous.
